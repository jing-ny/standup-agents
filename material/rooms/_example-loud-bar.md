# (the loud bar from the example show record)

## The room

Bar mic. ~12 comics, ~8 civilians, most of them half-listening over drinks.
Small corner stage, no real light rule — host waves from the bar. Sets run 3
minutes, list caps around 15.

## What this room rewards / punishes

Rewards short punchy lines and anything with a physical or vocal pop —
attention is bought one laugh at a time here. Punishes patience: any setup
longer than two sentences loses the back half of the bar. Warm crowd once
you've got them; brutal to win back once you haven't.
