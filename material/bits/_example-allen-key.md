# The Allen Key Is a Test

## Premise

IKEA furniture isn't a product, it's a psychological evaluation you pay to take —
and the allen key is the proctor.

## Draft

We bought a dresser from IKEA. The box says assembly time: 45 minutes.
It took us four hours. We are both college educated.

They give you one allen key. One. For a hundred and forty screws.
That's not a tool. That's a test.

Four hours in, my husband holds up the instructions and says,
"This piece doesn't exist."
It was in his other hand.

We finished with three screws left over. We didn't say anything.
We just... put them in a drawer. In the dresser.
So the dresser contains the evidence that the dresser is wrong.
It's been three weeks. Neither of us has mentioned it.
The dresser knows.

Everyone says IKEA is a marriage test. It is.
We passed — on a curve.

### Panel verdict — 2026-01-12 (draft v1)

| Lens | Grade | Top note |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | B+ | Punch word placement is right ("a test," "the dresser knows"), but the premise sentence never pays off — "psychological evaluation" is promised and abandoned. Either the closer callbacks the test frame or the premise is just the allen key beat's setup. |
| Truth+Pain | B+ | Truth is universal (everyone owns the leftover screws); pain is mild. The strongest pain in the dump — "we are both college educated" — is used as a throwaway. That's the humiliation engine; it deserves a beat, not a clause. |
| Voice/POV | B | "This piece doesn't exist / It was in his other hand" is the only beat with a specific human in it. The rest could be performed by anyone. Where is *your* reaction during the four hours? The POV is currently the dresser's. |
| Rhythm | A− | Reads as talk, not essay. "We just... put them in a drawer. In the dresser." lands on the ear. One flag: "contains the evidence that the dresser is wrong" is a written sentence — say it out loud and it'll shorten itself. |

**Consensus: B+ (untested)**
**Top fix:** the test frame opens the bit and never returns — either cut "that's
a test" or make the closer answer it (what grade did the marriage get, and who
was grading?). The fix is yours to write.
